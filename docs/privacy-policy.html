Privacy Policy — Margine

Last updated: September 2026

Margine is an informational app that displays sports betting odds from third-party bookmakers and calculates potential arbitrage opportunities between them. Margine does not process payments, does not place bets, and does not redirect you to place bets on any third-party site.

What we collect

  • Account information: when you sign in with email/password or with Google, we receive your email address to authenticate you. This is handled by Firebase Authentication (Google LLC).
  • App preferences: which bookmakers you've enabled or disabled are stored locally on your device (iOS UserDefaults) and are not sent to any server.

What we don't collect

We do not collect payment information, location data, or any data related to actual betting activity — Margine only displays publicly available odds data.

Third-party services

Margine uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Authentication (Google LLC) — for sign-in. See Firebase's privacy policy.
  • Google Sign-In (Google LLC) — optional sign-in method. See Google's privacy policy.
  • The Odds API — public odds data provider, no personal data is shared with this service.

We do not sell or share your data with any other third party.
